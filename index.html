Who We Are

Ground Zero Engineering Ltd is a premier EPC (Engineering, Procurement, and Construction) company. We specialize in integrated environmental projects, particularly in mechanical, civil, and electrical engineering.

With expertise in metal and structural fabrications, we provide comprehensive design and construction services for oil and gas storage facilities, including both surface and underground tanks.

Our Mission

To deliver unparalleled quality in project execution for clients across Nigeria. We provide customized solutions tailored to each client's unique needs, utilizing cutting-edge equipment and highly skilled professionals. We aim to build lasting partnerships by consistently exceeding expectations.

The Vision

To establish ourselves as the foremost leader in service delivery within the built environment of Nigeria. We aim to set the standard for excellence, driven by impeccable integrity and professionalism, contributing to the sustainable development of Nigeria’s infrastructure.
